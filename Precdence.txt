• P — Primary (Parentheses (), brackets [], member selection ->, .)
• U — Unary (++, --, +, -, !, ~, sizeof)
• M — Multiplicative (*, /, %)
• A — Additive (+, -)
• S — Shift (<<, >>)
• R — Relational (<, <=, >, >=)
• E — Equality (==, !=)
• B — Bitwise (&, ^, |)
• L — Logical (&&, then ||)
• A — Assignment (=, +=, -=, etc.)
• C — Comma (,)

P - Primary

U - Unary

M - Multiplicative

A - Additive

S- Shift

R- Relational

E- Equality

B- BitWise ( & > ^ > |)

L- Logical ( logical && > logical ||)

and the last three operators are

T- Ternary

A- Assignment

C- Comma
